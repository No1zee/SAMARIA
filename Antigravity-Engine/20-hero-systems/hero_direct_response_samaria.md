# Hero Template – Direct Response & Performance (Samaria-style)

## Intent

- Land like a high-converting landing page but still cinematic.
- Zero ambiguity on what the service is and what to do next.

## Inputs

- Core offer in one sentence
- Main proof point (experience, numbers, clients, outcomes)
- Primary CTA (book, call, get quote, start project)
- Risk reversal (guarantee, free audit, free call)

## Content order (desktop & mobile)

1. Eyebrow: category or trust tag (e.g. "Ambulance Network", "Web Studio", "Rugby Platform")
2. H1: clear promise
3. Supporting line: what you do + for whom + outcome
4. Social proof strip (logos or 1 concrete outcome)
5. Primary CTA button
6. Secondary CTA (view work, see how it works)
7. Microcopy under CTA about response time or next step

## Performance hints

- Keep LCP element simple: text + single image or gradient, not a heavy video.
- Defer non-critical animations; initial load should be mostly static.
- Limit above-the-fold fonts to 1–2 weights.

## Output format

Return:

- Hero copy in full
- Mobile variant (stack order, any copy tightening)
- Suggestions for 2 minimal motion touches that won’t hurt LCP
