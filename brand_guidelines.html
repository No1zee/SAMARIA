Samaria Tech Brand Guidelines

Engineering Africa's Digital Future

Version 1.0 February 3, 2026 Edward, Full-Stack Software Engineer Official Brand Standard

1. Overview

This brand guideline document establishes the visual identity system for Samaria Tech, an IT solutions company specializing in software design, automation, and bespoke IT ecosystems for African businesses. The purpose of this document is to ensure consistency, professionalism, and brand recognition across all digital and physical touchpoints as Samaria scales from word-of-mouth to market leader.

Brand Positioning: The premier architect of Africa's digital future—where ancient wisdom meets cutting-edge innovation.

2. Brand Strategy

2.1 Vision

To be the digital cornerstone of a borderless, technologically sovereign Africa, where ancient wisdom powers the innovations of the next millennium.

2.2 Mission

To engineer bespoke IT ecosystems and AI-driven solutions that automate progress, elevate African brands, and build the infrastructure for the continent's digital empire.

2.3 Core Values

  • Heritage-Driven Innovation — We look back at our roots to see further into the future
  • Architectural Precision — Like the stone-masons of ancient Samaria, our code and designs are built to last generations
  • Sovereign Intelligence — Empowering African businesses to own their data, their narrative, and their destiny
  • Bespoke Craftsmanship — No off-the-shelf thinking; every solution is a custom-carved masterpiece

2.4 Brand Motto

"By wisdom a house is built, and through understanding it is established; through knowledge its rooms are filled with rare and beautiful treasures."
— Proverbs 24:3-4

This scripture anchors our brand in timeless wisdom, strategic thinking, and the belief that technology should build lasting value.

3. Logo System

3.1 Logo Anatomy

The Samaria Tech logo system is designed to communicate both heritage and innovation through carefully balanced elements:

Primary Full Color Logo

Metallic Brass (#C5A059) on light backgrounds

The Samaria Tech wordmark features:

  • The Goddess Figure — A classical feminine silhouette crowned with a laurel wreath, representing wisdom, sovereignty, and ancient knowledge. She holds a laptop radiating light, symbolizing the fusion of timeless wisdom with modern technology.
  • Circuit Halo — Flowing circuit pathways arc above her, forming a modern halo that represents data flow, AI intelligence, and digital transformation.
  • SAMARiA Wordmark — Set in bold, wide geometric sans-serif typography with distinctive lowercase 'i', conveying strength, modernity, and architectural precision.
  • Color Palette — Metallic Brass (#C5A059) for the primary mark, representing African gold heritage and premium positioning.

Symbol Meaning:

  • Ancient Wisdom — The crowned goddess figure evokes classical African and Mediterranean heritage, referencing the ancient city of Samaria
  • Digital Sovereignty — The laptop symbolizes technological empowerment and modern solutions
  • Divine Innovation — The circuit halo merges spiritual enlightenment with AI/data intelligence
  • Bespoke Craftsmanship — Every detail is intentionally designed to communicate precision and excellence

3.2 Logo Lockup Variations

Full Color Lockup (Primary)

Complete Samaria logo with goddess figure and wordmark in Metallic Brass. This is the primary brand mark for all official applications.

Monotone Lockup (Secondary)

Single-color grayscale for simplified reproduction, embossing, debossing, or low-ink printing.

Full Color - Best for:

  • First brand introductions to new audiences
  • Website headers and primary navigation
  • Print materials (business cards, letterhead, brochures)
  • Official presentations and proposals
  • Large format applications (signage, banners)

Monotone - Best for:

  • Embossed or debossed printing (letterhead, business cards)
  • Single-color reproduction (stamps, seals, embroidery)
  • Fax, photocopy, or low-ink printing environments
  • Engraving on metal, wood, or glass
  • Black-and-white publications or newspapers

3.3 Logo Usage Guidelines

Clear Space: Maintain a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "S" in Samaria. No text, graphics, or competing elements should enter this zone.

Minimum Size:

  • Digital applications: 120px width minimum
  • Print applications: 1 inch (2.54cm) width minimum

What NOT to Do:

  • Do not change logo colors outside the approved palette
  • Do not apply drop shadows, gradients, or effects to the logo
  • Do not place logo on busy backgrounds that compromise legibility
  • Do not recreate or redraw the logo—always use official files
  • Do not rotate, stretch, or compress the logo disproportionately

5. Typography

Typography creates the visual contrast between Old World stone-cut elegance and New World futuristic technology, reinforcing the Neo-Ancient brand aesthetic.

5.1 Font Families

Role Font Rationale
Primary Headings Cinzel Decorative Stone-cut serifs reminiscent of palace inscriptions; elegant and timeless
Secondary Headings Orbitron Geometric, wide-set, futuristic; conveys space-age technology
Body Text Montserrat High readability, clean, professional; modern sans-serif

5.2 Type Scale Examples

H1 - Cinzel Decorative (48px)
The Digital Ivory Palace
H2 - Cinzel Decorative (36px)
Engineering Africa's Future
H3 - Orbitron (24px)
Bespoke IT Solutions
Body - Montserrat (16px)
We engineer bespoke IT ecosystems and AI-driven solutions that automate progress, elevate African brands, and build the infrastructure for the continent's digital empire. Our approach combines ancient wisdom with cutting-edge technology.

5.3 Typography Rules

  • H1 and H2 — Use Cinzel Decorative only for major headings; conveys authority and heritage
  • H3 and labels — Use Orbitron for subheadings and UI labels; balances tradition with modernity
  • Body text — Use Montserrat at minimum 16px for readability across all devices
  • Do not substitute fonts without brand approval—consistency is critical
  • Maintain clear hierarchy — Only one H1 per page; use H2, H3 for content structure
  • Line height — Set to 1.5–1.7 for body text to ensure readability

6. Design Elements

6.1 The Circuit-Tribal Motif

The Circuit-Tribal Motif is Samaria Tech's signature design pattern, merging traditional African geometric structures with modern circuit board aesthetics.

Visual Concept:

  • Motherboard traces forming the shape of traditional African shields, Mudcloth patterns, or Kente weaves
  • Hills of ancient Samaria integrated into digital pathways
  • Data nodes at pattern intersections representing AI intelligence and connectivity

Usage Guidelines:

  • Use in background layers at 10–20% opacity to avoid interfering with text readability
  • Apply to section dividers, hero illustrations, and page transitions
  • Limit to one major motif per viewport to prevent visual clutter
  • Do not place directly behind body copy—readability is paramount

6.2 Imagery Style

Element Treatment
Textures High-resolution macros of polished marble, raw earth, brushed gold, and circuitry

Icons Blend ancient motifs (columns, shields) with modern tech (circuits, data nodes)

6.3 Iconography

  • Automation — Gears integrated with circuit pathways

10. Brand Checklist

Before publishing any brand material, verify:

  • Logo is official file (not recreated)
  • Logo has adequate clear space
  • Colors match approved palette (Royal Obsidian, Metallic Brass, Ivory Glow)
  • Typography uses Cinzel Decorative, Orbitron, and Montserrat correctly
  • Text contrast meets WCAG AA standards
  • Circuit-Tribal motif is subtle and does not interfere with readability
  • Imagery aligns with brand aesthetic (heritage + technology)
  • Voice and tone match brand attributes (confident, heritage-aware, visionary)
  • All content is proofread and error-free
  • Accessibility requirements are met (alt text, semantic HTML, keyboard navigation)
